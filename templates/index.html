How It Works

From posting to decision

1

Create Profile

Students add their school, level, GPA, credits and a PDF or DOCX CV. Departments post positions with the GPA, credits, level and skills they need.

2

Screening

Each CV is read against the posting and given a score out of 100. Applications that clear the bar go to the department ranked by score, with a short reason attached.

3

Get Hired

When a department accepts or rejects you, you get a notification on your dashboard.
